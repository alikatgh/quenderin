Local voice on desktop

Choose an optional speech model during desktop setup, dictate into chat, and manage on-device transcription in Settings.

Quenderin’s desktop setup lets you add an optional speech model for dictation. The speech model turns your words into text. The local chat model you choose answers the message when you send it.

This guide describes the unreleased desktop source integration. Verification with a real microphone and full speech model weights is pending.

Set up voice

  1. Choose the local model you want to talk to in step 1. You can continue while the chat model downloads.
  2. In Talk to your local model by voice, choose a speech model or select Skip — I’ll type to leave voice off. The two English picks are Recommended — Parakeet Unified English 0.6B (697 MB), and More accurate — Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (2.0 GB).
  3. Start the speech model download with the button that shows its size, such as Download 697 MB, then select Finish setup. The picker says Downloads while you finish setup. You can pause and resume the transfer. Quenderin verifies the file’s SHA-256 before you can dictate with that model.

Compare speech models

Open Show all 29 models to search Handy’s curated on-device speech catalog. Live preview filters for models with streaming support and Translates filters for models with speech translation support.

ColumnWhat it shows
ENEnglish accuracy grade (A–F) based on Handy’s word error rate on the FLEURS test set. ? marks a missing measurement. — marks a model without claimed English support.
SPEEDMore bars mean a shorter estimated wait for 30 seconds of dictation on Handy’s AMD Ryzen 4750U GPU benchmark (Vulkan).
SIZEHow much you’ll download for the selected speech model.

Quenderin ran no inference benchmarks for this update. Speed on your computer depends on your hardware. See Handy’s published benchmarks.

Dictate into chat

When the speech model is installed and enabled, hold the microphone button in the chat composer and speak. You can also hold Ctrl+Shift+M or ⌘+Shift+M. Allow microphone access when prompted, then release to transcribe.

Your audio stays on your computer and transcribe.cpp turns it into text locally. The transcript appears in the composer, where you can edit it before sending it to the local chat model. The model downloads need a connection. With both models installed, local dictation and chat are designed to work offline.

Open Settings → Voice to pick a different speech model. Select Use this model if it’s already installed. Here you can also toggle Enable voice dictation or Automatically send transcript. If the app can’t open the microphone, check microphone permissions and the input device in system settings.
