Apple apps · available now

Your AI. On your Mac and iPhone.

30 September 2026 · Quenderin

Quenderin is available on the App Store for Mac and iPhone, free. You can install the native apps today, download an open language model, and have a conversation that runs on your own device.

That changes the first step. You no longer need Xcode or a terminal to get the Apple apps running. Get Quenderin from the store, choose a model that fits, and ask it something useful.

macOS iOS

One app for the desk. One for the journey.

On a Mac, there is room to work with longer drafts and explore the model library. On an iPhone, the same idea fits in your pocket: rewrite a message, brainstorm a plan, or ask for an explanation when you have no signal. Both apps are built natively in SwiftUI and run local inference through llama.cpp.

The model stays on the device. Your prompts and replies are processed locally. There is no account to create, no subscription, and no telemetry. Internet access is needed to install the app and download a model; it is not needed to generate replies after setup.

Start with one useful conversation

  1. Install the app. Use the Mac or iPhone App Store link above.
  2. Choose a model that fits. The app reads your hardware and recommends models. Start small if you are unsure; bigger downloads are not automatically a better experience.
  3. Download over Wi-Fi. Model files are separate from the app and can take several gigabytes. Wait for the model to be ready.
  4. Try a real task, then go offline. Ask it to rewrite a paragraph you wrote or explain a topic. Once it works, switch off the network and try again.

Local models come with real trade-offs

A phone has less memory and power than a data center. A smaller model loads more easily but can miss details or give weak answers. A larger model needs more storage and memory, and may take longer to respond. Devices, models, and prompts all affect performance; our measured results show the conditions behind the numbers.

These models can also be confidently wrong. Use them for drafts, explanations, and ideas you can check. Verify facts that matter. Privacy makes the conversation yours; it does not make every answer correct.

What we are building next

The next engineering work starts with the experience of using these apps: a model that fits, a clear download state, a responsive first reply, and useful answers on the hardware you already own. We will build on open-source inference work and measure improvements before calling them faster.
