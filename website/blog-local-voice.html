Desktop · source update

Talk to your local model by voice

27 November 2026 · Quenderin

Hold the microphone button, say what you want to ask, and release. Quenderin transcribes what you said on your computer and puts the words in the chat composer. You can fix a name or finish a sentence before sending it to the local model you chose. This is an unreleased desktop source update.

Two choices in desktop setup

First, choose the local chat model you want to talk to. The second step, Talk to your local model by voice, is optional. Pick a speech model there or choose Skip — I’ll type. You can set up voice later in Settings → Voice.

  • Recommended. Parakeet Unified English 0.6B is a 697 MB download and Handy’s faster English pick.
  • More accurate. Qwen3-ASR 1.7B is a 2.0 GB download with lower English word error in Handy’s results.

Open Show all 29 models to search the curated catalog or filter by Live preview and Translates. The table shows an English accuracy grade (EN), speed bars (SPEED), and download SIZE. The download continues while you finish setup, can be resumed, and is checked against the catalog’s SHA-256 before installation.

From the microphone to your draft

Once voice is enabled and the speech model is installed, hold the mic in chat or hold Ctrl+Shift+M (⌘+Shift+M on Mac). Quenderin asks for microphone access on first use. The existing transcribe.cpp runtime does the transcription locally, and the chat model receives the text when you send it.

Where the grades and speed bars come from

Handy’s results use the FLEURS test set for accuracy and the AMD Ryzen 4750U GPU with Vulkan for the speed bars. Those bars summarize the estimated wait for a 30-second recording on that hardware. Quenderin ran no inference benchmarks for this update. Real microphone capture and full-model inference verification are pending. See Handy’s published benchmarks.

We adapted the model picker and resumable download handling from Handy by CJ Pais and use its @handy-computer/models 0.2.3 package for the catalog, recommendations, and grades. Both are MIT-licensed. Handy on GitHub · Handy’s model package.
