0.6.1 build 35 is waiting for review

10 November 2026 · Quenderin

There is no account. A local model stays on the device. This note says what you can install today, what is waiting, and what is only on main. It does not say a model was run.

What is on sale

Checked on 10 November 2026, the App Store listing still offers Mac and iPhone 0.6.0, released 1 November 2026. Open the listing. That is the build on sale.

Waiting for Review

iOS and macOS 0.6.1 build 35 are Waiting for Review. They are set to release after approval. They are not on sale. You cannot download 0.6.1 from the App Store today.

The 9 November note still describes the on-device behavior in the source: a chosen model, a named note, one writer, a checker model, Look and Listen, local tools, Doctor, and Practice. This page does not say that behavior was tested as inference.

Play

Google Play still shows upload certificate 8b38f4e5. Nothing was uploaded. The public Play page is not a production listing, and Android remains a closed test.

Not in the store build

Security fixes are on main at 41c12e55. They limit how often the desktop service and its docs can be called, keep session and note files in their own folders, and pin patched Android build libraries. Those fixes are not in the store build, including the 0.6.1 build that is waiting for review and the 0.6.0 app that is on sale.
