## Short announcement

Your AI. On your Mac and iPhone.

Quenderin is free on the App Store for both. Download an open model once, then
write, learn, and chat offline. Your conversations are processed on your device.
No account, no subscription, no telemetry. Open source.

Get the app: https://quenderin.org/download.html

## Longer announcement

Quenderin is available on Mac and iPhone through the App Store, free.

Install the native app, choose a model that fits your device, and download it
over Wi-Fi. Once the model is ready, ask it to rewrite a paragraph, explain a
topic, or help with a first draft — even with the network off.

Local models have real limits: smaller ones can give weaker answers, while
larger ones use more memory and storage. Check important facts. Privacy keeps
the conversation yours; it does not make every answer correct.

Meet the apps: https://quenderin.org/blog-apple-launch.html

## Release wording

Apple apps: publicly available on the App Store. Windows and Linux: GitHub
previews. Android: closed beta on Google Play. Do not describe the Android
beta as a public launch, or the unrestricted CLI's full computer-control tools
as identical to the sandboxed Mac App Store app. Source changes to inference
are not a new App Store release until built, validated, submitted, and approved.
